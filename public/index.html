A LITTLE CHAOS BETWEEN FRIENDS.
WELCOME TO THE BOOP ROOM

Stack. Clear. Outlast.

Fill a horizontal row to clear it. Keep your stack below the top. The last Beepboop standing wins.

01 / Find Your People

Create a private room and share its link. Friends choose a nickname, ready up, and the host starts. Bots can fill empty seats.

02 / Send A Little Trouble

Double clears send 1 row; triples send 2; four-line clears send 4. T-spins, combos, and back-to-back clears earn extra rows.

03 / No Pile-Ons

Rows go to opponents with the least recent incoming pressure. Each row goes to one opponent. Nobody picks on one person.

04 / Push Back

Incoming rows wait 1.8 seconds. Your attacks cancel them first. At most 4 rows rise after a piece that clears no lines.

Everyone gets the same piece sequence. Gravity speeds up every 30 seconds. A disconnected player has 20 seconds to return while their board keeps falling. Live matches do not pause.
